Industry-Specific Accounting Solutions

One size does NOT fit all in accounting. We customize charts of accounts, revenue recognition, and tax schedules for your exact sector.

Sector 01

E-Commerce & Multi-Channel Retail

Managing payouts, merchant processing fees, shipping costs, and multi-state economic nexus thresholds across Shopify, Amazon FBA, Walmart, and WooCommerce is notoriously tedious. Prudent handles automated payout reconciliation and inventory COGS tracking so your net margins are 100% visible.

✓ Multi-channel fee reconciliations
✓ Economic nexus & sales tax returns
✓ FIFO/LIFO inventory valuation
✓ Advertising ROI & unit margin analysis
Sector 02

Healthcare, Medical & Dental Practices

Medical clinics, dental offices, physical therapists, and surgical practices operate under strict HIPAA regulations and complex billing cycles with third-party insurance payors. We provide practice-level bookkeeping, physician draw tracking, and medical supply expense control.

✓ Insurance reimbursement reconciliations
✓ Associate doctor production tracking
✓ Medical equipment lease vs buy analysis
✓ Practice cash flow budgeting
Sector 03

Real Estate & Property Management

For commercial developers, residential landlords, and property management firms, our accounting specialists structure property-level profit & loss statements, track security deposit escrow accounts, and compute depreciation schedules under current tax laws.

✓ Rent roll & CAM charge reconciliations
✓ Individual property P&L and Balance Sheets
✓ 1031 exchange transaction accounting
✓ Real estate professional tax status support
Sector 04

Legal Practices & Professional Services

Law firms face severe state bar sanctions if IOLTA client trust accounts are commingled or improperly reconciled. We specialize in three-way trust reconciliations, matter-level billing tracking, and partner equity distributions.

✓ Strict 3-way IOLTA trust reconciliation
✓ Matter billing & unbilled expense tracking
✓ Clio & PracticePanther QuickBooks sync
✓ Partner distribution & K-1 reporting
Sector 05

Tech Startups, SaaS & Digital Agencies

High-growth software companies require accrual-based revenue recognition (ASC 606), deferred revenue schedules, and investor-ready metrics. We track your MRR/ARR, net burn rate, runway months, and R&D tax credit eligibility.

✓ ASC 606 SaaS revenue recognition
✓ Net cash burn & runway modeling
✓ Federal R&D payroll tax credit capture
✓ Cap table & stock option accounting

Don't See Your Industry Listed?

We serve businesses across manufacturing, hospitality, non-profits, logistics, and consulting. Contact our team to review your specific accounting workflows.
